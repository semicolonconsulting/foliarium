# Privacy

Foliarium doesn't collect, store or send any personal data.

- It runs entirely on your Mac and makes no network connections.
- It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
- Your rules, its activity log and its settings stay on your Mac, in Foliarium's own folder in your Library.
- It reads and changes files only in the folders you choose in its rules.

If you have a question, open an issue at https://github.com/semicolonconsulting/foliarium/issues.
